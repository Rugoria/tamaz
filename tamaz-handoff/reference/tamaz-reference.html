Your 18-month journey

Scroll to watch a smile move into place.

Teeth move roughly one millimeter a month under light, steady force. Here is what that looks like from first scan to retainer.

  1. Scan

    Free consultation and 3D scan. About four minutes, with no impression trays.

  2. Plan

    Your orthodontist sets a target position for every tooth and shows you the result on screen.

  3. Bond

    Brackets go on in about an hour. You pick your band colors.

  4. Align

    The archwire gently pulls teeth into line. Adjustment visits every 6–8 weeks.

  5. Detail

    Fine-tuning of the bite, root angles and the last fractions of a millimeter.

  6. Retain

    Brackets off, retainers on. Your first set is included.

Questions

Before you book

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. It includes the exam, a 3D scan, any X-rays we need, and a written plan with pricing. There’s no obligation to start.

Do braces hurt?

Most people feel pressure and mild soreness for two or three days after bonding and after adjustments. Soft foods and an over-the-counter pain reliever usually cover it.

How often are appointments?

Braces patients visit every 6–8 weeks. Aligner patients usually come in every 8–10 weeks, and each visit takes 20–30 minutes.

What if a bracket breaks?

Call the studio. We keep same-week repair slots open at every location, and Saturday hours for school and work schedules.

At what age should my child be checked?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first check by age 7. Most children won’t need treatment yet, but early checks catch crossbites and crowding.
